Florida & Tennessee Land Prices Per Acre by County - Free Data

Free county-level land value data for all 67 Florida counties and all 95 Tennessee counties, compiled by Abundant Land Group from public federal datasets: the USDA NASS Census of Agriculture and the FHFA residential land value working paper. Download the CSVs below, or read the full studies with county tables and methodology:

  • Florida Land Prices Per Acre by County (2026) - full study
  • Tennessee Land Prices Per Acre by County (2026) - full study

Downloads (CSV)

  • fl_land_prices_per_acre_by_county.csv - Florida, 67 counties: farmland value per acre (USDA NASS 2017/2022) and FHFA residential land and quarter-acre lot values (2012-2022)
  • tn_land_prices_per_acre_by_county.csv - Tennessee, 95 counties: same measures
  • state_level_nass_land_values.csv - State-level farm real estate, cropland and pasture values, 2021-2026 (USDA NASS Land Values Summary)

Sources

  • USDA NASS 2022 Census of Agriculture, Vol. 1 Ch. 2 County Table 8 (via Quick Stats); 2017 Census of Agriculture
  • USDA NASS Land Values 2026 Summary
  • FHFA Working Paper 19-01 dataset, "The Price of Residential Land for Counties, ZIP Codes, and Census Tracts", v4.0 (June 2024)

Census values are farmer-reported market values of farmland including buildings, in nominal dollars. Counties marked n/a are not covered by the FHFA annual county panel due to insufficient appraisal samples. Free to use with attribution to Abundant Land Group and the underlying federal sources.
